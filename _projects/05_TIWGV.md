When we paused live performances in 2020, the Neos began making short films following the same weekly cadence and aesthetic principles as the stage show. I wrote and shot 40, and appeared in many more. 

My early pieces like _HAPPYWORK_ grappled explicitly with tedium and domestic isolation.
